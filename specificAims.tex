\section{Goal}
    This project aims to characterize the chemical footprint of global pollution in remote high-altitude environments by identifying unknown organic contaminants in snowmelt and river water using high-resolution mass spectrometry (HRMS).
    I hypothesize that long-range atmospheric transport leads to the accumulation of diverse and previously uncharacterized organic molecules in these regions.
    By combining non-targeted analysis with molecular networking, this study will reveal both the identity and distribution patterns of these contaminants.

\section{Specific Aims}
    \subsection{Aim 1: Identify and characterize unknown organic contaminants in high-altitude water samples}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item {\textbf{What (Aim):}} Use HRMS-based non-targeted analysis to detect and identify unknown organic molecules in snowmelt and river water collected from high-alpine regions.
            \item {\textbf{How (Approach):}} Water samples will be analyzed using liquid chromatography–HRMS, followed by computational workflows including molecular networking and suspect screening to annotate molecular features and cluster related compounds.
            \item {\textbf{Impact:}} This aim will generate a comprehensive chemical profile of contaminants in remote environments, moving beyond traditional targeted methods and uncovering “known unknowns” and novel compounds.
        \end{itemize}

    \subsection{Aim 2: Quantify spatial patterns and concentration gradients of detected contaminants}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item {\textbf{What (Aim):}} Determine the concentration and distribution of identified compounds across different sampling sites and environmental matrices.
            \item {\textbf{How (Approach):}} Relative or semi-quantitative analysis will be performed using HRMS signal intensities, with comparisons across locations.
            \item {\textbf{Impact:}} This aim will establish how contaminant levels vary geographically, providing insight into deposition patterns and environmental accumulation in high-altitude systems.
        \end{itemize}

    \subsection{Aim 3: Assess long-range atmospheric transport of contaminants}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item {\textbf{What (Aim):}} Evaluate the relationship between detected contaminants and atmospheric transport processes to determine how far pollutants travel from their sources.
            \item {\textbf{How (Approach):}} Chemical profiles will be compared with known atmospheric markers and literature data; patterns in molecular classes and volatility will be used to infer transport mechanisms (e.g., long-range atmospheric transport and cold trapping).
            \item {\textbf{Impact:}} This aim will link chemical presence to global transport pathways, improving understanding of how human-generated pollutants reach remote ecosystems.
        \end{itemize}
