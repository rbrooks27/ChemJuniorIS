\section{Goal}
    This project aims to characterize surface water samples from remote high-altitude environments by identifying both known and unknown organic contaminants using high-resolution mass spectrometry (HRMS).
    While database-driven approaches with lists of known contaminants enable identification of some contaminants, I hypothesize that a significant fraction of uncharacterized molecules can be identified through non-targeted analysis, particularly molecular networking.
    Together, these approaches will reveal the identity and patterns of contaminants in these remote regions.

\section{Specific Aims}
    \subsection{Aim 1: Preparation and Measurement of Surface Water Samples}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item {\textbf{What (Aim):}} Measure organic contaminants in surface water samples collected from remote high-altitude regions, including Mt. Everest, Mt. Kilimanjaro, the Tibetan Plateau, and Qinghai Lake.
            \item {\textbf{How (Approach):}} Samples will be analyzed using iterative data-dependent acquisition (DDA), where each sample is run multiple times to increase feature detection and improve coverage of low-abundance compounds.
            \item {\textbf{Impact:}} This aim will generate high-quality data with many mass spectral features, which will be subjected to identification and analysis of contaminants.
        \end{itemize}

    \subsection{Aim 2: Identify contaminants using suspect screening with the help of Agilent environment}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item {\textbf{What (Aim):}} Identify known environmental contaminants, pharmaceuticals, and other anthropogenic compounds using suspect screening (Agilent chemical database).
            \item {\textbf{How (Approach):}} HRMS data will be compared against curated chemical databases (e.g., Agilent database) using software such as MS-DIAL, leveraging exact mass and MS/MS spectral matching for compound annotation.
            \item {\textbf{Impact:}} This aim will establish a baseline of known contaminants present without additional identification efforts, allowing for comparison with non-targeted approaches. 
        \end{itemize}

    \subsection{Aim 3: Characterize unknown contaminants using molecular networking}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item {\textbf{What (Aim):}} Identify and characterize unknown organic molecules not captured by database screening using molecular networking approaches.
            \item {\textbf{How (Approach):}} MS/MS spectral similarity will be used to construct molecular networks, allowing unknown compounds to be grouped with structurally related known molecules. Mass differences and fragmentation patterns will be analyzed to propose potential structures and transformation products.
            \item {\textbf{Impact:}} This aim will fill critical gaps in contaminant identification by enabling discovery of novel compounds and environmental transformation products, significantly expanding understanding of chemical pollution in remote ecosystems.
        \end{itemize}
